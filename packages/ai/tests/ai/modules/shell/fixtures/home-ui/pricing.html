Subscription plans and metered billing, with no hard caps, no surprise bills, and no vendor lock-in. Pay for what you run and nothing you don't.

The billing unit

A token is a fixed slice of the CPU, GPU and memory your pipelines actually consume. It is not an LLM token. Every plan includes monthly tokens; beyond that, metered billing at ~$0.03 per token.
