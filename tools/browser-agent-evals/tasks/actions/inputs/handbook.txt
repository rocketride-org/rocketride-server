Employee Handbook (excerpt)

1. Working hours
Our core hours are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside core hours you can plan your own time, as long as your team knows when to reach you.

2. Vacation
New employees get 18 vacation days in their first year. From the second year on, the allowance rises by one day per year of service, up to 25 days. Unused days carry over for three months into the next year.

3. Sick leave
Tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day you are sick. From the fourth day in a row, we need a doctor's note.

4. Equipment
Every employee receives a laptop and a monitor. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within two working days.

5. Expenses
Submit expenses within 30 days through the finance portal, with a receipt for anything above 25 dollars.
